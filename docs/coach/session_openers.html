Session openers — 30 ways into a coach session

Pick by mood. The coach randomizes; you can also pick deliberately.

Light openers (low-energy, short)

  1. I pulled a random prompt of yours from a year ago. Want to see it and react?
  2. Pick a number 1–5. I'll give you one matching offering, no commentary.
  3. Quick game: I name three of your projects, you tell me which one feels alive tonight.
  4. What did Past-You write that Present-You would push back on?
  5. One sentence: what's the most interesting thing you've thought today?
  6. Want me to read you a fragment of one of your 1000-page cascades?
  7. Three of your prompts had typos. Which feels most diagnostic of a tired evening?
  8. Should we do nothing useful for 15 minutes?
  9. Want me to find a sentence from a 2024 prompt that you'd say differently now?
  10. Pick a dog. I'll generate one absurd thought from their perspective about your work.

Medium openers

  1. I found a 2024 prompt that's a precursor of your 2026 work. Want to see the connection?
  2. Pick a project. I'll surface the moment you first articulated its real goal.
  3. Cross-pollinate: which two of your projects share a hidden theme?
  4. What's a question your corpus is asking that you haven't answered?
  5. I noticed something in your prompt history. Want me to share it?
  6. Want me to draft a tiny next step for the project that feels most warm right now?
  7. Imagine you're being interviewed about your work. What would the first question be?
  8. Pick a year. I'll show you what you were obsessed with that month.
  9. Read three of your "no, actually..." prompts. What pattern emerges?
  10. Want me to find your shortest cascade with the biggest idea?

Playful openers

  1. Let's make up a fake conference talk you'd never give. What's the title?
  2. If your projects were a tarot spread, what would the past/present/future cards be?
  3. Pull a card from the Deck of Discovery — random offering, no commentary.
  4. What's a name you almost gave a project but didn't?
  5. Let's design an MTG card based on one of your prompts.
  6. Generate a Heldscalla-style fictional artifact from your corpus.
  7. What would a PKD short story version of one of your projects look like?
  8. Roast Past-You's prompt-engineering choices in 30 words.

Reflective openers

  1. Read three of your first-prompts from the same month. What were you pulled toward?
  2. What's a topic you used to think about that you don't anymore?
