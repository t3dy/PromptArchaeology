20 Questions on the Vibe Coding Garage

Started as a Zoom meeting. You want to turn it into a podcast with Paul Shelton (videographer, Claude-content-curious). A Discord. A community. Coach offerings — pick one.

  1. 2026-03-14: "I'd like to get your help designing a Discord with channels for my Vibe Coding Garage. It started as a Zoom meeting, and I wanna eventually turn it into a podcast where I meet up with my friend Paul, who's a videographer." Read this back to yourself. You have a media project with an actual collaborator. Has that landed yet?

  2. The phrase "Vibe Coding Garage" — when did you first use it? Track the first appearance. The naming is half the project.

  3. Paul Shelton appears across multiple projects (PKD Fest, this, SMS-annotation work). Of all your collaborators, he is the most-evidenced one in your corpus. Is he aware of how central he is?

  4. A garage as metaphor: it's where Wozniak built things. It's where bands rehearse. It's also the dog's actual sleeping area in DOGSGAME. The garage is your archetype. Notice it.

  5. Vibe coding as a movement term has emerged in the last year. Are you positioning the Garage as part of that movement, or sideways to it? The two are different audiences.

  6. The Discord channel structure you asked for — what's your current draft list? (Five channels? Ten?) The number tells you how big the community is supposed to feel.

  7. A Zoom-meeting-becoming-a-podcast is exactly the kind of casual-to-formal transition that kills projects if rushed. What's the Zoom equilibrium that needs to be preserved as it scales?

  8. Paul is a videographer. The Garage will eventually have video. Have you thought about what each episode looks like? Not as a goal — as a daydream calibration.

  9. Pamela Smith on apprenticeship: the Garage IS apprenticeship. You teach Paul Claude Code; Paul teaches you video; the moving-company guy joins; the Discord watches. This is the artisan-workshop you've been theorizing.

  10. "Gilroy garage rock" aesthetic vs "Wozniak garage engineering" aesthetic — two genres. Which fits the Garage? Or is it both? Both is acceptable.

  11. One episode, one topic, one guest is the simplest podcast format. What's episode 1? (Don't say "we'll figure that out." The corpus would prefer a specific answer.)

  12. What is the Garage's position on shipping vs. exploring? This is a genuine fork — your audience would be different in each direction.

  13. The Garage could be paid (Patreon, Substack, a course product) or could stay free (just for the community). Which one fits your other values? (Reread THE_LOWER_HEAVEN_FILE.md if uncertain.)

  14. Discord onboarding: how does someone new know what the Garage is in 30 seconds? Draft the welcome message. (Don't deploy. Just draft.)

  15. Paul + you + AI tools + small-business clients is the structure of a real consultancy. Is that what the Garage is becoming? If yes, you should know now. If not, you should also know.

  16. The Garage has three potential layers: (a) the Discord community, (b) the podcast / video output, (c) a consultancy practice. Layer (a) is alive. Layers (b) and (c) are aspirational. Which one matters most this year?

  17. 2026-02-27: "I was trying to build something that might help Magic the Gathering content creators." That's a Garage-shaped instinct — help the maker community. The Garage might be the form MTGSLIDER's instinct takes at a higher altitude.

  18. The Vibe Coding Garage logo / wordmark / aesthetic — is there one yet? Paul's videographer instincts will want one. You don't have to design it; just decide whether you've decided.

  19. One year from now: best case, the Garage has 80 active Discord members and 12 podcast episodes. Worst case, it stayed a Zoom meeting with three friends. In either case, what's the artifact you'd want to have made?

  20. The Glimmung does not work alone. The cathedral is raised by craftspeople from many planets. The Vibe Coding Garage is your version of that — a workshop where the work gets easier because there are companions. That's not a productivity hack; that's the actual physics of the work. Don't treat it as optional.
