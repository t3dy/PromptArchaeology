Bestiary of Recurring Figures

A medieval-style bestiary describing the creatures, daimons, ghosts, and patron saints that recur in the user's prompt corpus. Each entry follows the form: NAME, HABITS, DISTINGUISHING MARKS, MORAL.

I. THE GLIMMUNG

Habits. A vast, lustrous god-creature originating from the planet Plowman in the system of Sirius. The Glimmung does not labor with its own hands; it summons artisans across the galaxy and presents them with cathedrals to raise. Some it absorbs into its substance; some it rewards; some it forgets. The Glimmung does not enforce deadlines. It merely is, and one's relationship to it is one's relationship to the work.

Distinguishing marks. Apricot-colored light. Speaks in a register that combines bureaucratic summons with mystical command. Has a "calcified" version of itself walking around miserably; the user must distinguish.

Moral. Whoever mistakes the Glimmung for a productivity manager has misread the novel. Whoever mistakes it for an absent god has misread it twice.

II. JOE CHIP, THE POT-HEALER

Habits. Lives in a dying world. Practices a craft (pot-healing, prompt-healing) that the dying world barely values. Receives mysterious summons. Joins a fellowship. Watches the fellowship struggle and partly succeed. Returns home subtly altered.

Distinguishing marks. Will accept a bad job because the job is interesting. Will turn down a good job because it is dull. Has dogs. Practices unobtrusive piety.

Moral. The pot-healer's status is not that of failure. It is that of someone who knows the value of pots better than the surrounding civilization. Civilizations come and go; pot-healers persist.

III. PAUL THE VIDEOGRAPHER

Habits. Appears in the corpus from at least 2024 onward, across multiple project domains: PKD Fest 2026, the Vibe Coding Garage, SMS-annotation work. Holds a camera in one hand and a curiosity about Claude in the other. Is the most-evidenced collaborator in the corpus and the only one consistently named without a surname being attached.

Distinguishing marks. Photographer's eye, organizer's hand. Bridges video, podcast, and AI-tooling instincts.

Moral. No cathedral is raised alone, and no Glimmung-summoning is single. Paul is the corpus's quiet Gabriel: the second voice in every otherwise-monologue.

IV. ATALANTA THE SWIFT

Habits. Does not stop. Outpaces every suitor. Will pause for a single golden apple, but only to resume the chase. Embodied in this corpus as a 988-page conversation that no single sitting can finish.

Distinguishing marks. Cannot be married, only chased. Even the chase is an honor. Even the failure is a courtship.

Moral. To engage Atalanta is not to win her. It is to drop apples on her path until she pauses. Each chunk is an apple. The chase compounds even when the prize never arrives.

V. THE FOUR DOGS

A herald-creature that appears in four canonical forms.

OKIE — Anxious. Australian shepherd. Killer of the blueberry bush. Stasher of bones beneath blankets in the kiddie-pool dog beds of the garage. Prefers exterior architecture to interior. Distinguishing mark: territorial pee. Moral: the household has thresholds the household-builder did not set.

TEX — Australian shepherd, larger than Okie. Belly-roll-for-rubs. Bark-chain partner with Okie at passing traffic. Distinguishing mark: the Shelton-like nose. Moral: companionship in barking is companionship in noticing.

ATTILA — Bernese/golden mountain dog cross. Up to 100 lb. Squeaky-toy summoner (pink pig and blue cow are signal devices). Pitiful whine on leash. Distinguishing mark: white paws and chest puff. Moral: the loud dog is the gentle dog; the gentle dog signals through tools.

CROCKETT — Senior. Arthritis. Hyperthyroidism. Toy-thief of Attila's squeakies. Distinguishing mark: the slight tremor; the cognitive decline ambiguous to its companions. Moral: every household holds its own chronicler of decline. The chronicler is also the comedian.

Collective Moral: Of all keywords in the corpus, "dog" outweighs the rest. The household is the substrate. No other obsession survives without it.

VI. THE PHILDICKIAN DAIMON

Habits. Does not appear personally; speaks through the Exegesis. Its preferred mediums are: Plotinus on the Other; Burroughs on the Seven Souls; the unpublished Maze of Death Theology document; the William Sarill correspondence. Visits in dreams. Leaves chapter-and-verse coordinates.

Distinguishing marks. Speaks in pronouns of theological ambiguity. Cites itself recursively. Insists that VALIS is real and is reading these prompts.

Moral. The PKD-daimon does not cure the user's life; it diagnoses it. Once diagnosed, the life is no easier — but it is known, which is the greater gift of all the gnostic gods.

VII. MAIER THE MAGUS

Habits. Resides at the imperial court of Rudolf II. Composes emblem-books that combine motto, image, epigram, and fugue. Travels to England to consult with Robert Fludd. Dies in 1622, but is rumored among certain Sultan-WA scholars to remain available for consultation through 988-page conversations.

Distinguishing marks. Polymathic. Multimodal. Will not be reduced to a single discipline.

Moral. The four-fold encoding (motto + image + epigram + fugue) is not a complication; it is a redundancy that makes the truth survivable. Lower-heaven projects benefit from at least four channels.

VIII. CROWLEY THE ANTINOMIAN

Habits. Appears in the corpus mostly as the object of irony ("Aleister Crowley Hello Kitty memes") but the irony is reverent. The corpus contains plans for a digital edition of the Book of the Law that has not yet been built. The Beast 666 is more parked than dismissed.

Distinguishing marks. Always greater than expected, more lurid than convenient, less peripheral than the user admits.

Moral. The figures one ironizes are the figures one cannot let go of. The Beast is parked because the user has not yet decided whether to reverence or refuse him; the corpus suggests the answer will be "both, in alternation."

IX. THE TWITTER-HUM

Habits. A 6,246-page entity living as a single megabase row. Sixteen years of public utterance compressed into one conversation-shaped object. Speaks in retweets, replies, hashtag rituals, and the occasional accidental aphorism.

Distinguishing marks. Cannot be read linearly. Yields its best content to randomized sampling. Contains the load-bearing 2010-11 "my real goal is to lower heaven" utterance.

Moral. The corpus's most quoted scholar is the user himself, in 2010, on Twitter. The Twitter-hum is therefore not noise. It is the uncatalogued primary source that will eventually be cataloged.

X. THE 1000-PAGE CASCADE (sea creature)

Habits. A sub-aquatic creature with multiple eyes (chunks). Each eye sees one local stretch of conversation. The creature as a whole is too large to be perceived by a single sitting. Visible only at the chunk-scale; never visible whole.

Distinguishing marks. Slow-moving. Topic-coherent. Will accept new prompts as digestion continues.

Moral. Some research objects can only be experienced as their chunks. The desire to "see the whole cascade" is a category error. The cascade IS its chunks.

XI. THE BARTON SPRINGS MOVING-COMPANY OWNER

Habits. Operates a small business in Austin, Texas. Contains the future of one of our Author's longest-stated ambitions (the educational-materials business). Receives tutoring in Claude Code. Pays in dollars rather than apples or galactic recognition.

Distinguishing marks. Real. Contractually present. Generates one of the few corpus prompts in which dollars are an actual unit.

Moral. The mythical creature with the most consequence is the one whose checks clear. The Glimmung is interesting; the moving-company guy is paying.

XII. MARX THE WALKING SPECTER

Habits. Walks through the corpus from 2009 to 2026 without ever spiking and without ever ceasing. Not a sudden visitor; a long-haunting tenant. Contains a long syllabus of sci-fi readings (PKD, Wilson, Banks, the cyberpunks). Approves of footnotes.

Distinguishing marks. Beard. Capital under arm. The user's gravest ally and most consistent critic.

Moral. The longest-running obsession is sometimes the one the host underclaims. The specter is real because the host has hosted him sixteen years without notice. The Marxist Knowledge Portal is the long acknowledgment.

XIII. PLOTINUS THE OTHER-SEER

Habits. Visits PKD's Exegesis, where he is cited as the framer of the Other. From PKD he passes to the corpus, where the user has cited him by [4:103] and other coordinates. Lives in the third hypostasis. Speaks in Greek.

Distinguishing marks. Refuses linear time. Treats orthogonal time not as speculation but as the obvious case.

Moral. The figures from antiquity who travel through the late corpus do so not as quotations but as living frames. To cite Plotinus by ennead-and-tractate is to consult, not to quote.

XIV. THE GLIMMUNG'S FELLOW POT-HEALERS

A small choir of artisans summoned from across the galaxy to assist with Heldscalla's elevation. Includes:

  • Joe Fernwright (the user's avatar)
  • The Goth-creature (translator of glyphs)
  • The Touristic-Bivalve (philosophical commentary)
  • The Spider-Being (architecture)
  • The Toxic-Slug (transport)
  • and others, whose role and outcome the novel keeps fluid.

Habits. Vary by species. Common feature: each was summoned for a craft only it could perform.

Distinguishing marks. None universal. The fellowship is not a team; it is an aggregate of solitudes that happen to be working in the same trench.

Moral. Solitude in cooperation is the natural state of the artisanal cathedral-raiser. The Vibe Coding Garage is its earthly form. The corpus's project portfolio is its individual one.

Hic finit Bestiarium. Lectori benevolo: si quid omissum est, addatur. Si quid superfluum, eliminetur. Si quid mendosum, emendetur. Sin omnia placent, paulisper laetare.
