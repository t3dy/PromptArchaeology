The Tediganus Set — A Spoiler Card-By-Card

A custom Magic: The Gathering set in which each of the user's projects, recurring figures, and methodological moves is rendered as a card. Drafted in the spirit of pre-release preview season. Designed to be playable in casual EDH; designed more to be funny.

MYTHIC RARES

Heldscalla, the Sunken Cathedral                        {2}{U}{B}
Legendary Land — Cathedral

Heldscalla enters the battlefield tapped and submerged.
{T}: Add {C}.
{T}, raise a counter from Heldscalla: Add {U}{B}.
At the beginning of each upkeep, raise one counter
on Heldscalla for each artifact you control with
"Restored" in its name.
When Heldscalla has 50 raise counters, transform it.

"He did not know if it would rise. He kept working."

Joe Chip, the Pot-Healer                              {1}{W}{U}
Legendary Creature — Human Artisan

{T}: Return target Artifact card from your graveyard
to your hand. It gains "Restored" if it didn't already
have it.
Patient (Pay {1} during your upkeep: Joe Chip phases out
until your next end step instead of attacking. Repeat freely.)

"Some of the pots had stories. He read them slowly."
2/4

The Glimmung, Cathedral-Summoner                {3}{W}{U}{B}{R}{G}
Legendary Creature — God Alien

When The Glimmung enters the battlefield, summon up to
five creatures with different colors from your library
to the battlefield. They have haste until end of turn.
At the beginning of each end step, exile a summoned
creature. Its controller loses the game if it was theirs.

"He does not enforce deadlines. He simply is."

Megabase                                              {3}
Legendary Artifact

Megabase has eleven counters on it when it enters.
{T}: Look at the top card of any library. You may put

messages / segments / ideas / entities).

"The river that catches every other river."

MTGSLIDER, Slide Compiler                             {2}{W}{U}
Legendary Artifact — Engine

{T}, exile a card from your hand: Create a slide deck —
a colorless artifact token with "{T}: Reveal a card from
exile. Whenever you reveal a card this way, draw a card."
Etymology trigger — Whenever you reveal a card, you may
also reveal a corresponding alchemy token.

"Take the serious source. Add the gloss of casual play."

NSFRIPPER, the Athanor                              {2}{B}{R}
Artifact — Equipment

Equipped creature has "{T}: Choose a noise channel —
pulse, triangle, or DPCM. Add {C}{C} of that color."
Equip {3}.

The triangle gates off, the DAC holds.
The bug becomes the rule. The rule becomes the vow.

Atalanta the Swift                                  {1}{R}{G}
Legendary Creature — Human Hunter

Haste, vigilance.
Whenever Atalanta would be blocked by a creature with
power 2 or less, you may have its controller draw a card
to remove it from combat.
"Cascade" — Whenever you cast Atalanta, exile cards from
the top of your library until you exile a creature card
with mana value 988 or less. You may cast it for free.

The Exegesis (Bound Edition)                       {U}{B}{R}
Legendary Enchantment — Manuscript

Pages — The Exegesis enters with five page counters.
At the beginning of your upkeep, you may pay {1} and
quote a chapter and verse: draw a card and discard a

"4:103. The Other is not any one thing found in any

a specific color."

Set design notes: Tediganus draft archetypes by color pair —

  • {W}{U} — Restoration / Patience (Joe Chip, Heldscalla, Q-Log)
  • {U}{B} — Cascades / Reading (The Exegesis, Megabase)
  • {B}{R} — Athanor / Iteration (NSFRIPPER, the bugs become rules)
  • {R}{G} — Atalanta / Hunter / Speed (Atalanta, Voice-Mode Walk)
  • {G}{W} — Apprenticeship / Garage (Vibe Coding Garage, Pamela Smith)

The set is balanced for casual play. The Glimmung is the format-defining mythic and should be banned in 60-card formats. Heldscalla is legal everywhere — it just doesn't usually rise in time.

— Tediganus Lead Designer, 2026
