Emblemata Tediganī

An emblem book in the Maierian register. Each of the user's projects is a hermetic emblem with motto, image, epigram, and discourse. After Michael Maier, Atalanta Fugiens, Frankfurt 1617 — and after sixteen years of prompt-corpus rehearsal of the form.

Emblem I — Heldscalla emergit

Motto: Cathedra sub aquis, ferenda emergit. — The cathedral beneath the waters rises by being borne up.

Image: A submerged stone basilica, half-overgrown with coral. A small craftsman in a diving bell carries a single repaired pot toward the choir. Above him, far off, the hull of a Glimmung-ship hovers, casting an apricot light through fathoms of water.

Epigram:

Where archives drown, the patient hand Lifts shards from silt and reads the brand; No single pot raises the nave — Yet the nave rises, wave by wave. What is a cathedral's stone foundation But many vessels in conversation?

Discourse: Ye who would understand the Work, observe well the Galactic Pot-Healer of Dick: he raises not the cathedral by lifting it, but by restoring its parts. The whole that emerges is not the sum of stones but the patience of stoners. So too with our Author, whose corpus exceeds 1.45 million prompts: he raises Heldscalla not by sealing the breach but by re-glazing the bowls. Magnum opus est patientia.**

Emblem II — Tabula vincit chaos

Motto: Ad mensam quamlibet rem reducere. — To reduce any thing whatsoever unto the table.

Image: A scholar at a stone tabletop has spread a map of correspondences: Plato's dialogues across one axis, six Neoplatonists across another. Each cell is a ruby cabochon. Behind him, a screen with a cursed scroll-bar weeps unctuous oil onto the floor.

Epigram:

Three Platos, six commentators, and a Cratylus; And lo, the Hermetic corpus shall sit between us. No scroll bar shall darken this table's mouth — By the rule of design, it points always SOUTH. The mensa is the medium; the medium, the work; Whoever masters the rows masters the murk.

Discourse: On 19 October 2024 our Author iterated the same prompt seven times, demanding that the table accept its width without recourse to the despicable scroll-bar. Beneath this petulance lay a Manifesto, viz., that the table is not the Apparent Goal but the Actual Object of Study. He who builds tables for hermetic corpora is not building an interface — he is practicing the principle of containment by which all knowledge becomes pourable.

Emblem III — Schedae per saecula natant

Motto: The shards of every prompt swim through every age.

Image: A river of paper currents flowing from 1994 (a single trickle) to 2026 (a delta in flood). Each currentlet is labeled: ALCHEMY, PKD, MTG, MARX, CHIPTVNES, CANIS. The waters merge in a great basin called MEGA・BASIS upon whose lip a green parrot reads aloud.

Epigram:

Twenty-six years of murmured prose, No archive sleeps — each datum knows The shape of the next year's obsession. The corpus is its own confession. When you forget what you have written, The base remembers, undivulgéd, smitten.

Discourse: Megabase (the unified corpus, 5,271 conversations, 11 sources) is the catchment basin of our Author's flowing thought. Note the conjunction: where most archives are dammed and lose their living current, this one remains aerated by reading-of-the-self. The prompt-archaeologist who studies it is not embalming — he is, as the Greeks said, ζωοποιεῖν — making alive again.

Emblem IV — Athanor in Octavo Bit

Motto: The athanor heats in eight-bit registers.

Image: A blacksmith's forge with the chimney-flue routed through a Famicom motherboard. Sparks come out as square waves. The smith is striking a pulse-wave anvil; behind him, eight thousand nine hundred MIDI scrolls hang from rafters like cured ham.

Epigram:

The triangle gates off, the noise relents, The DAC holds: this is the Work's true sense. Not loud, not quiet — the in-between Where all great chiptunes are foreseen. Each bug a rule, each rule a vow, Rule thirty-eight is broken — now.

Discourse: Our Author has prosecuted the labour of NSFRIPPER through 321 game-extractions and 8,900 MIDI artifacts. The two DSP codebases (stems and JSFX) drift apart like the twin pillars of the Temple, each more virtuous in its absence than its presence. The athanor's principle: the heat that does not unify is doing the wrong work. Either fuse the codebases or declare them brothers and let them argue. Twins in tension are still twins.

Emblem V — Atalanta vincit, fugiens vincitur

Motto: Atalanta wins by fleeing; the fleeing one wins.

Image: Atalanta racing across a printed page; the suitor's apples (golden) lying scattered as page-numbers (988). A scribe in the lower margin tries to count the apples; behind him, three more scribes compose a fugue.

Epigram:

The 988-page Cascade unrolls And catches up with him who scrolls; Read one chunk in evening light — The work outpaces appetite, But what you cannot finish whole, You may yet finish in the soul.

Discourse: The Atalanta Fugiens Cascade is among the deepest single-topic conversations in our Author's corpus. To attempt it whole is to chase Atalanta — futile. To read it in chunks of fifty prompts is to drop golden apples in her path: she stops to lift each one. Lectio per pomos is the prescribed method.

Emblem VI — Fons quaestionis

Motto: The fountain of questions runs upward.

Image: An inverted fountain — water rises from a pool back into spouts and into the air, each spout labeled with a question. A young scholar holds a vessel beneath the air to catch falling questions and pour them downward into a memorandum book.

Epigram:

What I am actually trying to do Arrives by Prompt the Forty-Two. Not the first ask, nor the second guess, But the candid third confession's redress. The fountain runs against gravity: It is the question, not the answer, that frees.

Discourse: The "what I'm actually trying to do" reveal is a hermetic moment, occurring (per the Author's corpus) typically around the fifth or tenth prompt of a substantive conversation. It is not produced by the AI. It is produced in the user by the AI's failure to satisfy. The dialectic is constitutive. To call it failure is to misread the genre.

Emblem VII — Canes vigilant noctu

Motto: The dogs keep watch at night.

Image: Four dogs of distinct breeds arranged on a yard's slope, in postures of attention. Above them, written across the night sky in dim emblematic stars: OKIE, TEX, ATTILA, CROCKETT. A blueberry bush lies dead and triumphal in the foreground.

Epigram:

No corpus is complete without the canine — The household life beneath the sign; The prompts that mention dogs by name Outweigh the prompts of any fame. In every alchemy a domestic load: The Work is also fed and walked the road.

Discourse: In our Author's data, "dog" mentions in the SMS sources spike to 2,123 in a single month (July 2022) — the all-time peak of any single keyword in the entire corpus. The household is the substrate. No DOGSGAME engine, no Atalanta cascade, no PKD knowledge portal can be properly understood except as overlaid upon four real dogs in a real Sultan, WA, yard.

Emblem VIII — Operarius cum Glimmungo

Motto: The artisan with the Glimmung.

Image: A workshop. At the bench, two figures: a Maker (Paul, the Videographer) holds a film-camera; a Teacher (Joe Chip, the Pot-Healer) holds a Claude-Code terminal. Between them, sparks visible as small text: vibe coding garage. Above the bench, painted in mock-Latin: APPRENTITIVS NON SOLITARIVS.

Epigram:

No cathedral rises by one hand, No grimoire is signed by single brand. The Garage holds three crafts in trust: Cinema, terminal, conversation just; Whoever apprentices to its forge Becomes both pot-healer and Glimmung-gorge.

Discourse: The Vibe Coding Garage is the most evidenced-but-least-realized project in the corpus. Paul appears across multiple project domains; the Garage subsumes them. The artisanal-epistemology framework of Pamela Smith (uploaded by our Author 18 February 2026 and never explicated in subsequent prompts) is its theoretical underpinning. Smith on artisans-as-knowers IS the Garage. The Garage is what an early-modern alchemist's workshop would look like in 2026 if its members had a Claude subscription.

Emblem IX — Mercator petens scholam

Motto: The merchant who seeks instruction.

Image: A moving-company truck whose trailer is open to reveal not furniture but books. The driver, in seventeenth-century apparel, has dismounted to ask the way of a Schoolmaster. The Schoolmaster (our Author) extends a single Claude Code terminal in payment.

Epigram:

The mover came to Austin's gate And asked the way to automate; No SaaS could fit, no app sufficed — A bespoke teaching he was priced. In Smith's old phrase: "the artisan knows" — Now mover and tutor join repose.

Discourse: Barton Springs Moving entered the corpus 12 March 2026 as a "potential gig"; the contract was won the next day. This is the educational-materials business prefigured in 2015 finally arriving with a customer's name. The Schoolmaster sells not software but sufficiency. The mover becomes apprentice; the truck becomes a teaching-vehicle.

Emblem X — Dictionarium PKDianum

Motto: The Dictionary of Phildickian Things.

Image: A great folio open on a lectern, its pages exhibiting the names of Dick's recurring concepts: DARK-HAIRED-GIRL, KASCH, IT, VALIS, TAGOMI, UBIK, SCANNERLY-SELVES. A cat (gray) sleeps upon the index. Above the lectern, in Greek: ΠΛΩΤΙΝΩ ΕΝ ΔΙΑΛΟΓΩ — In dialogue with Plotinus.

Epigram:

He spent four-thousand-and-eight-twenty-four Prompts in one month to settle the score; Plotinus' Other and Phil's Self meet On Exegesis four colon one-oh-three. No fan has cited it like our Sage — The Concordance unfolds upon the page.

Discourse: In August 2025 our Author logged 4,824 prompts on Phildickian topics — the largest single-month obsession in the corpus by a factor of three. He cites the Exegesis chapter-and-verse, an academic move rare among PKD readers and still rarer among PKD fans. The QueryPat / PKD Knowledge Portal is the inevitable artifact of this devotion. The wonder is that it has not yet been built; the certainty is that it will be.

Emblem XI — Spectrum capitalis

Motto: The specter of capital walks.

Image: A 19th-century specter (top hat, beard, coat, Das Kapital under arm) walks along a path that becomes, in successive panels, a Discord channel, a podcast feed, a Substack, a Reddit thread.

Epigram:

The specter does not stop appearing Through every platform's brief endearing; No spike, but constant low-watt hum — Marx is the bass beneath the sum. When other obsessions cool or quicken, The Specter still walks, though he grow stricken.

Discourse: Marxist concerns appear across every quarter of the corpus from 2009 to 2026 without significant gap. Unlike alchemy or PKD which spike, Marxism abides. It is the throughline our Author would not name first when asked his commitments — but the corpus would. The Marxist Knowledge Portal (announced 12 March 2026) is therefore not a pivot. It is the long acknowledgment.

Emblem XII — Ipsius corpus relectum

Motto: The body of self, re-read.

Image: A scholar reads from a great book whose pages are mirror-glass. In each page he sees himself reading. Above him, an angel with one hand on the book and one finger to its lips: fac quod legis — do what you read.

Epigram:

To distant-read the self in time Is more than therapy or rhyme; It is the medieval art of reditus — The soul's return to its first impetus. What thou hast typed in fatigue or zeal The mirror-page makes pure and real.

Discourse: The instrument of these emblems — Promptarchaeology-Heldscalla itself — is the methodological emblem of all the others. By reading his own corpus, our Author becomes both subject and analyst of an unusual longitudinal study. The mirror does not flatter; nor does it lie. The Glimmung does not decide whether the cathedral rises. He provides the diving bell.
