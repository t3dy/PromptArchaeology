Twenty-five tweets pulled from the parallel-universe Twitter where you actually wrote down what your prompts already imply. Voice modeled on your real timeline (2010–2024) and your prompt cadence (2024–2026). None of these are tweets you wrote. All of them are tweets your corpus says you should have. Use as you wish — including not at all.
