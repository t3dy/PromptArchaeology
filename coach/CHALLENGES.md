# Challenges — 25 bounded games to play with the corpus

Each challenge has a bound. Most fit in 30 minutes. None require finishing anything beyond the challenge itself. The bound is part of the gift.

## Tiny (under 15 min)

1. Quote yourself — find an old prompt of yours you'd want to read at a podium.
2. Pithy precursor — find your shortest prompt that contains a big idea.
3. Find the typo — surface a prompt with a typo that's also revealing.
4. One emoji per project — pick one emoji that captures each active project.
5. First sentence — write one sentence about your work that would make a stranger curious.
6. Steal from Past-You — find a sentence from a 2024 prompt to use in a 2026 doc.
7. The 8-word self-portrait — describe your work in exactly eight words.
8. The lost vocabulary — find a word you used in 2023 that you don't anymore.

## Small (15–30 min)

9. Cascade arc — pick a 100+ prompt cascade. Read prompts 1, 25, 50, 75, 100. Note the arc.
10. Three projects, one theme — find a theme that's in three of your projects but unnamed.
11. The fork in the road — find a moment where you almost went a different direction in a project.
12. The redirect cluster — read 10 of your "no, actually..." prompts. What pattern emerges?
13. Etymology of a project — find the first time a project's name appears in your prompts.
14. Underline three — read 30 random first-prompts. Underline three you'd write again.
15. The unstated assumption — find a prompt where you assumed something you'd now spell out.

## Medium (30–60 min)

16. The lost project — find a project you started in your prompts and never built. Decide: revive or eulogize (in writing).
17. Translate yourself — take a 200-word stretch of one of your prompts and translate it into haiku.
18. The voice match — find prompts of yours from two different years on the same topic. Note voice differences in 100 words.
19. Single chunk — read one chunk of a 1000-page cascade. Write three sentences about what you notice.
20. Concept birth — pick a key concept in your work. Find when it first showed up in your prompts.
21. Shadow project — find a project that exists in your prompts but not in your filesystem.

## Multi-session

22. Cathedral floor — read one chunk of the Atalanta Fugiens cascade per evening for a week.
23. The hundred-first — read your 100 most recent first-prompts in one sitting. Notice patterns.
24. The redirect anthology — curate 30 of your best "no, actually..." moments into a single page.

## Absurd

25. The pantheon — assign a god, saint, or PKD character to each of your projects. Defend each match in one line.
